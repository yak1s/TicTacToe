How to Play

1. The Grid: The game is played on a giant 3x3 board, where each square contains a smaller 3x3 board.

2. The Catch (The Core Rule): You do not get to pick which small board you play in. Your opponent's previous move dictates your current board.

➜

Playing in the top-right square sends your opponent to the top-right board.

3. Winning a Board: Get 3-in-a-row in a small board to win it. That board counts as a giant X or O for the main game.

4. The Wildcard: If you are sent to a board that is already won or completely full, you get a free turn! You can play your next move anywhere on the giant board.

5. Winning the Game: Win 3 small boards in a row on the giant board to win it all!
